Journey

My school journey

The most significant event of my journey was changing schools. My fresh start dates back to March 2024: I'm convinced that, without that choice, I probably would not have continued studying. I changed because I no longer felt motivated and was struggling to keep going — I needed an environment where I felt at ease and could start over.

The transfer was an important turning point, not just academically but personally too: it gave me new motivation and new opportunities. Another significant moment was the third-year work placement, during which I learned first-hand how a warehouse operates, its logistics and organisation — aspects that connect directly to my dream: creating my own clothing brand.

Among the most positive aspects are the people: friendly and supportive teachers and friends who have been there for me during difficult times. One of the biggest challenges was learning how to communicate with adults, a problem that began back in high school. Thanks to my experience at Immaginazione e Lavoro, I worked on this, gradually improving my confidence and interpersonal skills.

Every experience, positive or negative, has helped me grow. My future goal is to create a clothing brand, and to get there I would like to do internships in Digital Marketing, E-Commerce or as a Graphic Designer.
